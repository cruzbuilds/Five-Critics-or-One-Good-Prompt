# Adjudication ledger

The running account of judging findings: what I decided, what was hard, and what I got wrong. Append
only. Newest at the bottom. Earlier entries are never edited.

`verdicts.csv` is the machine-readable record. This file is why each verdict went the way it did,
written while deciding rather than reconstructed afterwards.

## Rules I am holding myself to

- Arm A's list is scored in full before arm B's is opened.
- Source labels are stripped before scoring.
- Nothing is a true positive on my reading alone. It needs corroboration: a `tsc` error, an eslint
  rule, an audit advisory, a failing test, or a reproduction anyone can run.
- Anything I cannot corroborate and cannot confidently judge goes to **unverifiable** and never enters
  a total.
- When I want a finding to be real because it would help my hypothesis, that is worth writing down
  here, in that sentence, and then judging it on the evidence anyway.
